# -Superstore_Sales.csv
This dashboard presents a comprehensive view of sales, profit, discount, and quantity across various categories, regions, and markets.
•	Profit Distribution: Technology leads with the highest profit, followed by Office Supplies and Furniture.
•	Sales Trends: Sales are distributed across multiple categories and regions, with a noticeable peak in certain years.
•	Discount Analysis: Furniture has received the highest discounts, influencing profitability.
•	Regional Performance: Different regions showcase varying levels of sales and profit contributions.
•	Market Insights: Sales and profit distribution vary across order IDs, states, and specific markets.
